# 🏓 PICKLERS — Brand Guide & Visual Identity Manual

This document records the exact visual identity standards for the **PICKLERS** application.

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Mission
- **What is PICKLERS?** A premium web application built for the pickleball community. Its primary purpose is to be a comprehensive platform for both players and facility owners, eliminating the friction usually involved in finding courts, connecting players, and managing facilities.
- **Mission:** To fuel the growth of the pickleball community by building a premium platform where players can instantly book courts and join open matches, while equipping facility owners with an error-free dashboard to manage scheduling, payments, and staff.

---

## 2. Official Color Palette

```
  Mint Green         Dark Navy          Sky Blue
   #55C39E            #0A121F           #69A2D0
 (85, 195, 158)     (10, 18, 31)     (105, 162, 208)
```

- **Mint Green (`#55C39E`)**:
  - Primary Brand Color
  - Primary CTA Buttons ("Book a Court")
  - Active Sidebar Pill Tabs
  - Main Brand Title Text
- **Dark Navy (`#0A121F`)**:
  - Primary App Surface & Background Color
  - Dark text on Mint Green pills & buttons
- **Sky Blue (`#69A2D0`)**:
  - Secondary Accent Color
  - Secondary CTA Buttons ("Join Open Play")
  - Secondary Logo Mark Element

---

## 3. Typography Rules

- **Logo & Primary Brand Name ("PICKLERS"):**
  - Font: `Montserrat`
  - Weight: `ExtraBold` (`800` / `900`)
  - Use: Reserved exclusively for the main "PICKLERS" brand text.
- **Primary Headings & Section Titles:**
  - Font: `Inter Variable`
  - Weight: `Bold` (`700` / `800`) or `Semi-Bold` (`600`)
  - Use: Large website banners, modal titles, and section headers.
- **Body Text:**
  - Font: `Inter`
  - Weight: `400` (Regular) / `500` (Medium)

---

## 4. UI Elements & CTA Standards

- **Primary Button ("Book a Court"):** Solid `#55C39E` background with `#0A121F` dark navy text.
- **Secondary Button ("Join Open Play"):** Solid `#69A2D0` background with `#FFFFFF` or `#0A121F` text.
- **Active Navigation Items:** Solid `#55C39E` rounded pill button with `#0A121F` dark text and icon.
- **Background Shell:** Deep `#0A121F` Dark Navy.
